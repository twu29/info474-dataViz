We built a scrollytelling data visualization that explores over 120 years of Olympic data, from the first modern Games in Athens 1896 all the way to 2024. Our intended audience is anyone curious about Olympic sports history or global trends.

As you scroll through our article, you can explore questions like: Which countries have dominated the medal counts, and has that changed over time? How has global participation shifted across 120 years? And what does the age distribution of medalists tell us about peak athletic performance?

Data source: https://github.com/KeithGalli/Olympics-Dataset?tab=readme-ov-file
